In short: LOOT JACKAL LOOT! is fully playable without creating an account and without providing any personal data. If you choose to sign in with Google or Discord, we collect the minimum needed to create your profile and sync your saved games across your devices. If you post a score to the leaderboard, other players see the nickname you chose and your score, and nothing else. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Kappo Studio & Mangiapalazzo Studios, developer of the LOOT JACKAL LOOT! application.

For any question about this policy, or to exercise your rights, write to salvommiranda@proton.me.

2. What data we collect

If you play as a guest

No personal data. Your game progress is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app, that data goes with it.

If you sign in with Google or Discord

Signing in is optional: it is only needed if you want to recover your progress on another device. When you sign in, your chosen provider (Google or Discord) passes us the basic information from your profile, which we store to create and manage your account:

DataWhy we need it
Email addressTo uniquely identify your account and let you recover it
Name or nicknameTo display your profile inside the game
Profile pictureTo display your avatar inside the game
User identifierTo link your saved games to your account
Game progressTo sync your game across your devices
Leaderboard nicknameTo show you on the public leaderboard — see below

We never ask for or receive your Google or Discord password: authentication happens entirely at the provider, which only shares the information listed above.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device files, microphone or camera.

The online leaderboard

Signed-in players can submit their score to a public leaderboard. When you do, two pieces of information become visible to every other player:

  • the nickname you chose inside the game;
  • your score.

Nothing else is shown: not your email address, not your real name, not your profile picture. The nickname is one you type yourself and is separate from your Google or Discord name, so you can stay anonymous simply by choosing a nickname that does not identify you. We recommend you do.

Submitting to the leaderboard is voluntary. Nicknames pass through an automatic filter that blocks offensive words, and other players can report a nickname they find inappropriate; we remove or reset entries that break the rules. If you want your entry removed, write to salvommiranda@proton.me, or delete your account, which removes it as well.

Guest players cannot submit scores and never appear on the leaderboard.

4. Who else processes your data

We rely on a single provider for the service infrastructure:

  • Supabase — provides the authentication and the database that store your account and saved games. It acts as a data processor, meaning it processes data solely on our behalf and on our instructions. Data is hosted on servers located in Frankfurt.

In addition, Google and Discord process your data as independent controllers when you use their services to sign in. To understand how, please read their respective privacy policies.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data with anyone. The only information visible to other people is the nickname and score you choose to publish on the leaderboard.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and saved games for as long as the account exists. When you request deletion, the data is erased within 30 days of the request.

If your account goes unused for a very long period, we may delete it to free up resources, giving you advance notice at the associated email address.

6. How to delete your account and data

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Full instructions are on the dedicated page: Account deletion. Alternatively you can write directly to salvommiranda@proton.me.

If you play as a guest there is no account to delete: simply uninstall the app.

7. Your rights

As a data subject under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 you have the right to:

  • access the data concerning you and obtain a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • obtain erasure of your data;
  • restrict processing in certain circumstances;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format (portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

To exercise these rights, write to salvommiranda@proton.me. We respond within 30 days.

If you believe the processing of your data breaches the law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

8. Children

LOOT JACKAL LOOT! is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account, write to salvommiranda@proton.me and we will delete the account and its data.

9. Security

Communication between the game and our servers takes place over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Database access is protected by rules that allow each user to read and modify only their own data. No system is 100% secure, but we apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we process data, we will update this page and the date at the top. For substantial changes we will notify you inside the game. We encourage you to review it from time to time.
